not merely the range count. I also inspected the CONT-04 resolver and its full-corpus controls for

transitive links and cycles. Those controls run in the required suite. The new tilde-fence probe
shows a defect before reference normalization: the enclosing section is truncated, so neither the
rest of the fence nor its required link reaches that resolver. This limits confidence in all 110
closures; I do not claim the sampled historical sources themselves contain that exact tilde shape.
