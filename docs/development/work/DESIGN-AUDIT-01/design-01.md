## Mechanism

This is a read-and-probe audit. Its payload lives here; only this packet's row in 007
may change outside this directory. No product source, maintained test, Layer-3 rule,
implemented-baseline claim, historical record, or process document changes.

DA-1 will read Git objects, not a moving checkout. For K0.1, K0.2, K1.0 and their
corrections, and archived K1.1/correction-01/reference-01, use
`9fd2faa71bc4e2b7b4798c53a0360b669c5d7b49`. For K1.1-correction-02 and K1.2 and
correction-01 use the audit base. Include nested independent-review records and
finding-bearing supplements/cleanup records; separate their provenance from review
authors. Enumerate every finding-ID occurrence with source revision, path and line.
Produce a deduplicated ID table, retaining references, aliases and repeated mentions
rather than counting each mention as a fresh finding. Record non-blocking observations
separately; do not silently discard unprefixed or unusual IDs. Cross-check headings,
findings tables and explicit no-findings reviews against the extraction. A checked-in
classification map assigns each ID a primary category (design/evidence/records),
subsystem, family and defining record. The script must fail on an unclassified ID or
an unused mapping. Its table will state the extraction grammar and exceptions.

Families follow the mechanism, not merely the spelling of an ID. Select every family
with at least three distinct findings or findings originating in at least two rounds.
A later mention/closure alone does not create a new round. Keep the source-ID mapping
visible so a reviewer can challenge grouping and the qualification denominator.
Register entries cover every selected family plus suspect items (a)–(f), with cross-links
where a shared mechanism applies. Each entry gets a draft decision, not an adopted one.

Read current governing rules through `mental-model/reference.md`: values for capture,
identity and execution-cycle for exchange/receipts, authority for grants, state/evidence
for retained observations, operations/resources for Trusted and Isolated Execution.
Historical reviews supply counterexamples and provenance, not replacement semantics.
