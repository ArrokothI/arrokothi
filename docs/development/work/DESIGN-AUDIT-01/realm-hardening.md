A startup identity check cannot prevent a later binding swap. Review 02 observed this on v25.2.1; round 3 runs the same probes on the installed Node v26.8.1, with versions printed rather than equated.

## Truthful hardening draft and future gate

A-hard/B-hard now require clean bootstrap before uncontrolled initialization, permanent non-writable/non-configurable own bindings for isNaN, isFinite, Object, Array, JSON, Set, Error and Symbol, plus freezing and verification of the entire relied-on intrinsic graph. Audit every adapter/dependency read. Local capture in the adapter alone cannot change the unmodified dependency's free global reads. A genuinely closed dependency environment would require its own evidence; no serializer fork is proposed here.

The replacement must reject unsupported setup before accepting requests, preserve coherence and all ordinary ownership/atomicity obligations, and perform no per-call prototype/intrinsic/global writes. Every mid-capture binding attempt must fail or have no effect. In the probe model, permanent pinning makes all eight assignments throw and preserves ordinary canonical output; the recorded prototype-comparison bypass disappears. This demonstrates the binding mechanism only. The flag-only control fails the integrity gate even after a successful startup identity check.

Positive Kernel canonicalization and complete-decision compatibility are a separate necessary gate. The current Kernel still accepts the eight ordinary controls unfrozen and refuses them under the flag with `unstable_representation`: its `withSerializerEnvironment` setup attempts writable/configurable definitions on frozen holders. Therefore current flag-only configuration **fails both the complete binding-integrity gate and positive Kernel availability**. Unsupported inputs may refuse earlier for other reasons. No hardened Kernel support is counted by this audit.

The alternative draft would narrow V-ENV integrity as well as availability to stable cooperative realms; that costs the named ambient-guarantee amendments. Neither alternative is adopted. Hardening does not bound Proxy/getter execution, prove coherent snapshots, classify hidden Proxy targets, meter traversal or contain native capabilities. CORE's hardened column and K11C03/K14 dispositions carry these same conditions. Both holds remain.
