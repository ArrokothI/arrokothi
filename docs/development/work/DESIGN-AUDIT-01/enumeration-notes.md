# Enumeration method and reconciliation

`enumerate.py` discovers pinned review/cleanup/handoff/owner-note Markdown by path, hashes every source, extracts ID occurrences, and requires exact equality between discovered labels and the classification manifest. `--check` detects missing classifications and stale generated tables. `review-sources.json` also lists no-finding records. Review numbering is not assumed continuous: missing correction reviews 05/07 are not created.

The primary classification is the cause of the claim failure: design for behavior/rule/representation, evidence for a check or oracle that does not establish its claim, records for provenance/status/current prose. Subsystem and category are currently assigned at family granularity; they are not independent classification axes. A behavior bug can have an evidence consequence without being counted twice. Qualifying families use review findings, owner supplements and cleanup findings; self-references and observations are additional coverage, not inflated independent review rounds. The origin round is curated; recurrence mentions do not add rounds.

Local `SELF-*`, `CORR2-SELF-*` and `O-R*` labels are packet-qualified; bare O1/O2 labels are source-qualified. SELF-DIAG-01/02 and SELF-R6-HOST-01–03 ranges are expanded. CATALOG-01 is explicitly retained. Criteria, decisions, probe names and mutation labels are evidence, not finding IDs; the evidence catalog retains their source records. Meaning cells sometimes preserve a short source excerpt rather than reconstruct an unavailable self-finding report; follow the definition/reference locator for the full claim.

Aliases remain visible. K11-R15-VAL-01/DISP-01 were rekeyed to R16; R3I authority/history are the same K1.2 R3 findings; the short DISPATCH spelling and repeated local observations are reconciled in `alias_of`. K11-R15-ID-01 and DOC-01 were also reused by different reviewers: the original creation/input collision and cumulative-documentation findings keep those IDs; the second review's revoked-Proxy and atomicity/durability findings are represented under the subsequently assigned R16 IDs. This is a provenance collision, not extra independent discoveries. The table's source links preserve the evidence for reconstruction.

`hostile_only` in the manifest means the motivating distinguishing failure needs active same-process behavior (including coherent Proxy observations deliberately disagreeing with ambient serialization), or an enforcement rule specifically created for that defense. It does not mean every line implementing the repaired invariant can be deleted. Snapshot identity, own `__proto__` preservation, bounded diagnostics, ordinary reentrancy and atomic state remain valuable with cooperative callers. Findings outside that flag are not all cooperative: many concern process/evidence instead.

Completeness is for the declared pinned records and their explicit labels, not undisclosed conversations. Unlabeled prose-only P3 observations are outside the ID table and are not comprehensively enumerated in the evidence catalog. The review-01 severity-line search locates them without inventing historical IDs. K0.1-process-review is excluded because it audits process rather than the specified Kernel packets; its PRC labels are outside this declared universe. Historical dispositions remain at their original records; this audit groups causes and recommends follow-up, without re-reviewing or reversing all historical verdicts.
