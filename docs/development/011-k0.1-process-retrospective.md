# K0.1 process retrospective

Inspection baseline: `42731300266eea00a9a24d867d5e82d9887c280d`, 2026-09-11. K0.1 required twelve
implementation candidates, eleven recorded CHANGES REQUIRED verdicts and one independent ACCEPT.
The reviews prevented real defects from becoming accepted requirements. Nevertheless, the history
shows avoidable serial discovery: corrected rules were not propagated through their dependent paths,
and some supposedly settled areas were not challenged against their full observable consequences.
Twelve rounds were neither all administrative overhead nor demonstrated to be irreducible discovery.

Retain the C → H → A → owner integration skeleton. Materially redesign universal prompts as short
role launchers, with authoritative policy and selected proof methods outside them. The main remedy
is better coverage and correction closure, not simply fewer words, another reviewer or a round quota.
This is the author's forensic judgment and process-change candidate, not independent acceptance of
its own redesign. K0.2 remains unimplemented and unreleased.

## Evidence and limits

Inspection covered AGENTS.md, docs/README and mental model; development README, 001 and 006–010;
the complete K0.1 contract/worksheet, every implementation-01–12 and review-01–12, and all round-3
evidence files including their scanner. Connecting Git inspection covered all 36 C/H/A commits,
their file scopes, successive contract/worksheet changes including superseded rules, cumulative
content and integration. Historical narratives were checked against the changed rules, not treated
as independent proof of the implementer's interpretation. Relevant canonical protocol, acceptance,
cancellation and recovery passages and the cited legacy cancellation code were inspected to understand
the findings. This was not a new Kernel architecture audit or a rerun of historical test commands.

The records do not expose every chat, private reasoning, actual context utilization or elapsed effort.
The owner reports a primary reviewer considered a candidate acceptable before another reviewer found
the cancellation gap. The repository preserves the latter as [review-11](work/K0.1/review-11.md),
not an additional primary H11 ACCEPT. Do not invent that missing review's identity or coverage.
Model comparisons and an exact counterfactual round count cannot be inferred from this history.

## Judgment on self-review, independent review and packet size

Implementation self-review was substantial but insufficient. I6–I8's consistency tables often use
section presence, phrase occurrences or unchanged text as evidence; these checks help detect editing
errors but cannot establish the shared semantics. I7 even flags the routing reading later corrected
by R7. I8 leaves a required deadline case open. By contrast, I9's pre-edit scenario reconstruction
finds several additional consequences before handoff. The remedy is to require that kind of reasoning
at first implementation and after semantic changes, including connections beyond the latest cluster.
A known owned semantic gap must stay IN_PROGRESS or use the appropriate blocker, never be handed to
the reviewer under WAITING_FOR_REVIEW.

The old reviewer prompt already required cumulative review (009 Prompt B step 6) and named cancellation,
waits, identity, evidence and many other areas (step 7). Therefore adding “review everything” is not
a solution. The records assert cumulative inspection; they do not prove that reviewers stopped after
one finding. What they lack is a consistently visible independently derived account of negative space:
which interacting paths and losing-side mutations were challenged, and what remained unexamined.
Repeated “accepted and closed” lists also made preservation of previous answers more salient than
challenging their dependencies. New review records require coverage evidence and continued examination
after the first defect, without claiming any finite list guarantees correctness.

The second-review episode supports a coverage/assumption correction, not compulsory multiple models.
An independent reviewer must derive expected behavior from canonical sources and trace all mutations,
even if the report says a mechanism already satisfies it. Additional review is justified for a named
coverage, expertise or access concern; several partial verdicts must not silently become acceptance.
No evidence here proves the same reviewer could never find the cancellation gap using that method.

K0.1 was broad: equality, identity, batch/wait/time, cancellation, compatibility and legacy disposition
interact before executable fixtures exist. C1–C4 largely inventory boundaries, sections and vocabulary;
C5/C6 require coherence, but did not operationalize a whole-protocol audit. C6's self-contained worksheet
and detailed migration assertions encouraged repeated normative text. The packet's structure therefore
contributed materially to the burden. Yet splitting waits, timeouts and batches into separate packets
would merely move the same integration defect. Keep coherent units; expose interaction obligations and
split only independently observable claims with a combined gate. No retrospective packet redivision or
K0.2 contract release is made here.

## Prompt abstraction and responsibility changes

A common entry point remains useful for discovery, actor selection, identity and stop conditions.
A universal all-purpose proof script is not the right abstraction. The old 009 has 209 lines / 2,160
whitespace-delimited words, while 006/008 repeat much of its policy. More strikingly, the worksheet
rises from 486 lines / 6,088 words at C1 to 2,511 / 35,013 at C12; the contract from 115 / 1,133 to
301 / 3,626. Review-09 alone is 1,242 lines, including historical handoff reproduction. These counts
show accumulating reading and maintenance load; they do not prove a context-window failure. The
specific stale rules and incomplete propagation are stronger evidence than size alone.

## Substantive work versus administrative transport

The reachable history contains 12 payload commits, 12 report/status candidates and 12 review/status
commits before the merge. H3 additionally contains nine evidence files including a script. All other
H commits are report plus ledger; A commits are review plus ledger. Separate hashes are justified:
they expose what was tested, what was submitted and what the independent reviewer accepted. Do not
collapse these into self-referential or moving candidates to reduce turn count.

All eleven corrective cycles contain substantive decisions or consistency repairs. Evidence/provenance
repairs coexist with them in R1–R3 and R9; they are not eleven administrative-only rounds. Report01
also admits a force-pushed early draft, contrary to the then-current policy; later preserved C/H/A
ancestry is verifiable, but that admission is not independent evidence of the remote's full past state.
The available repository cannot count every owner/chat transport turn. Avoidable effort includes
retyping findings/policy, inaccessible log references, embedding repeated log exceptions and stale
current status. Essential work includes source inspection, counterexamples, independent verdicts,
validated immutable candidates and verification of merge equivalence. One post-merge receipt can
record integration and the already-supplied owner discussion without another approval ceremony.

## Application beyond this packet

Specification-heavy K0.1 especially needed exact grammar, all value forms and prose consistency.
Executable packets gain deterministic oracles, but can still encode the same wrong assumption in
implementation and tests. K0.2 needs independent controls and evidence-owner distinctions; K1 needs
observable refusal and full write-set assertions; K3 needs actual fault windows and surviving evidence;
R1 needs native-side observation; S1 needs the packed consumer. A blanket protocol worksheet for all
of them would repeat the universal-prompt error. 012 selects methods without altering milestone scope.

Do not promise a target round count or automate semantic acceptance. Small mechanical checks can
verify links, identities and scope; they cannot derive cancellation correctness. The proposed method
should be evaluated at K0.2 and the next executable packet: which defects were self-found, which
first appeared in review, which were adjacent consequences missed after a correction, and what
handoff burden remained. Preserve rejection quality and mandatory evidence when judging improvement.
There is no result from that future experiment yet and no release granted by describing it.
